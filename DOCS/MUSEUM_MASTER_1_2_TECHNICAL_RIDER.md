# Gallery and auction-house technical rider

## Required presentation

- 77-inch-class 4K OLED or equivalent professional 16:9 display; no specific hardware purchase is represented.
- Native 3840×2160 input, 100% browser scaling, overscan disabled, stable network, surge protection, and a serviceable browser-capable player.
- Approved immutable or stable Preview URL only. Production and private operator interfaces are outside the exhibition scope.
- Wall Art Mode for public display; fullscreen entered and exited by a trained attendant.

## Daily opening

1. Power network, player, and display in that order.
2. Confirm the approved branch, commit, URL, and 16:9 uncropped frame.
3. Require `living_wall_truth.v1`, `AVAILABLE/CURRENT`, `telemetry_read_only=true`, and ledger/write/trade/live-execution false.
4. Confirm all eighteen rooms, MAX, collector marking, and safety strip are visible.
5. Select the appropriate brightness profile, enter Wall Art Mode, then enter fullscreen manually.

## Supervision and incident behavior

- Never disable browser, operating-system, or panel OLED protection.
- Pause Scene or use reduced motion for photography, accessibility, maintenance, or any motion concern.
- `STALE`, `SOURCE_CONFLICT`, `UNAVAILABLE`, Safety Lock, clipping, unexpected authority, or an unknown URL is a stop condition. Keep the architecture visible, exit public presentation if necessary, and escalate to the designated operator.
- Do not expose developer tools, response bodies, credentials, browser history, notifications, or private telemetry.

## Daily closing

Exit fullscreen, close the approved browser window, stop only the local wall-display process if one was started, shut down the player cleanly, and follow the panel manufacturer's power guidance. Record interruptions and recovery in the exhibition log.
