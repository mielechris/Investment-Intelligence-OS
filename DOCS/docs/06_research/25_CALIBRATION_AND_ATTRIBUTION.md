# Calibration and Attribution

## Confidence Calibration

For every confidence-bearing component:

- agent;
- committee;
- thesis score;
- strategy.

Measure observed outcomes by confidence bucket.

## Calibration Metrics

Where appropriate:

- Brier score;
- log loss;
- reliability curve;
- expected calibration error.

## Attribution Layers

Separate:

1. event identification;
2. evidence quality;
3. causal reasoning;
4. agent interpretation;
5. committee decision;
6. risk sizing;
7. execution;
8. market outcome.

## Process Attribution

A profitable trade may still have:

- weak evidence;
- wrong causal explanation;
- poor sizing;
- lucky execution.

## Outcome Attribution

A losing trade may still have:

- sound evidence;
- correct expected-value decision;
- adverse variance.

## Learning Rule

Update trust in the part of the pipeline that failed, not the entire system indiscriminately.
