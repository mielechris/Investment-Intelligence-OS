# Research Promotion and Retirement

## Promotion Criteria

A strategy may move to paper monitoring only if it passes:

- hypothesis registration;
- point-in-time validation;
- baseline comparison;
- out-of-sample test;
- realistic cost test;
- sensitivity test;
- regime analysis;
- reproducibility;
- risk review.

## Promotion Record

Must include:

- strategy version;
- research runs;
- known weaknesses;
- approved use;
- paper limits;
- owner;
- review date.

## Pause Triggers

- data-quality failure;
- performance drift;
- calibration failure;
- model dependency changed;
- regime shift;
- operational defect;
- unexpected drawdown.

## Retirement Triggers

- persistent out-of-sample failure;
- no value over baseline;
- excessive cost sensitivity;
- unstable parameters;
- unacceptable drawdown;
- invalid causal assumption;
- data source no longer valid;
- market structure changed.

## History

Retirement never deletes prior results.
