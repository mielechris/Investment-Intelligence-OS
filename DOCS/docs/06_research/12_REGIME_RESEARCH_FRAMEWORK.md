# Regime Research Framework

## Purpose

Determine when a strategy works and when it fails.

## Regime Dimensions

- growth;
- inflation;
- liquidity;
- central-bank stance;
- credit;
- volatility;
- dollar;
- commodity pressure;
- risk appetite.

## Required Analysis

For each strategy:

- performance by regime;
- drawdown by regime;
- turnover by regime;
- hit rate by regime;
- calibration by regime;
- transition-period behavior.

## Transition Risk

Strategies may fail during regime transitions even if they work within stable regimes.

## Point-in-Time Regimes

Historical regime labels must use only information available at the historical cutoff.

## No Cherry-Picking

A strategy cannot be promoted only for the regime where it happened to look best unless the regime-selection rule itself is tested.
