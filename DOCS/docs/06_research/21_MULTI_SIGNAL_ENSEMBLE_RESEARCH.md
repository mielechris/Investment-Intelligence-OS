# Multi-Signal Ensemble Research

## Objective

Determine whether independent signal families improve decision quality when combined.

## Signal Families

- policy;
- macro;
- geopolitical;
- weather/commodity;
- corporate;
- flow;
- market structure;
- valuation;
- regime.

## Independence

Signals must be evaluated for shared origin.

Ten news articles repeating one source are not ten signals.

## Combination Methods

Research may compare:

- hard rules;
- weighted scores;
- logistic models;
- tree models;
- calibrated probabilities;
- Bayesian combinations;
- agent committee outputs.

## Required Baseline

Compare against:

- best single signal;
- equal-weight signals;
- simple scorecard.

## Overfitting Control

Do not tune dozens of signal weights on one historical period without holdout.
