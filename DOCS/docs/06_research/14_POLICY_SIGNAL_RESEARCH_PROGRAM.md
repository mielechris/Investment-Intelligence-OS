# Policy Signal Research Program

## Objective

Test whether public presidential, executive, legislative, regulatory, judicial, trade, and implementation events contain repeatable market information.

## Event Classes

- presidential remarks;
- White House meetings;
- executive orders;
- memoranda;
- proclamations;
- tariff announcements;
- formal tariff implementation;
- agency rules;
- congressional votes;
- committee actions;
- court rulings;
- government contract awards;
- industrial-policy announcements.

## Critical Distinction

Separate:

```text
rhetoric
→ announced intent
→ formal action
→ legal effectiveness
→ implementation
→ realized economic effect
```

## Research Questions

- Does implementation stage predict stronger reaction than rhetoric?
- Are beneficiaries concentrated by sector?
- How quickly does market pricing occur?
- Does market reaction reverse?
- How important is pre-event positioning?
- Does Fed/macro context dominate policy?
- Do official meetings without formal action have predictive value?

## Controls

- broad market;
- sector;
- rates;
- dollar;
- concurrent macro events;
- earnings;
- commodity moves;
- market regime.

## Required Counter-Test

Shuffle event dates and compare against matched non-event days.
