# Research Constitution

## 1. Research Exists to Falsify

The default objective is to find why a strategy may fail.

A strategy that survives hostile testing earns trust.

## 2. Point-in-Time Integrity

No future information may enter historical features, labels, universes, classifications, policy states, revisions, or decisions.

## 3. Benchmark Everything

Every complex strategy must be compared with one or more simple baselines.

If complexity does not add measurable value, the simpler method wins.

## 4. Costs Are Real

All execution-sensitive research must report both gross and net performance.

## 5. Failed Research Is Preserved

Negative results, abandoned variants, and invalid hypotheses remain in the registry.

## 6. No Hidden Multiple Testing

The number of strategies, parameter combinations, and variants tested must be recorded.

## 7. Holdout Means Holdout

Repeated inspection of a holdout converts it into a development set.

## 8. One Good Backtest Is Not Proof

Promotion requires multiple forms of evidence:

- historical;
- out-of-sample;
- robustness;
- regime;
- forward paper.

## 9. Public Strategy Reconstruction Is a Hypothesis

Observed trades or holdings do not reveal hidden hedges, discretionary overrides, or exact execution.

## 10. Process Before P&L

A profitable result with invalid research process is not evidence of edge.

## 11. Confidence Must Be Calibrated

A 90% confidence label that wins 55% of the time is miscalibrated.

## 12. No Guaranteed Wealth Objective

Research is judged by repeatable edge, risk control, and decision quality, not by a predetermined account-value target.
