# Macro and Federal Reserve Research Program

## Objective

Test how central-bank actions, expectations, inflation, labor, growth, liquidity, and rates affect asset returns.

## Event Classes

- FOMC decisions;
- minutes;
- speeches;
- testimony;
- inflation releases;
- labor releases;
- growth releases;
- liquidity changes;
- Treasury issuance;
- yield-curve changes.

## Surprise

Separate:

- raw release;
- expectation;
- surprise;
- revision;
- market reaction.

## Research Questions

- Which sectors are most rate-sensitive by regime?
- How does surprise magnitude relate to returns?
- Does policy rhetoric matter less than market-implied expectations?
- When do rate cuts become risk-off rather than risk-on?
- How does dollar response change commodity effects?

## Required Point-in-Time Data

Use first-release macro vintages where available.
