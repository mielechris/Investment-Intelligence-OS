# Public Flow Research Program

## Objective

Test whether delayed public holdings, insider activity, futures positioning, fund flows, short interest, and buybacks contain useful information.

## Sources

- public institutional holdings;
- insider transactions;
- futures positioning reports;
- ETF/fund flows;
- public short-interest data;
- buyback disclosures.

## Required Lag Modeling

Use:

- measurement date;
- transaction date where known;
- publication date;
- market-available date.

## Research Questions

- Does a new public position predict future returns after publication?
- Are clustered insider buys more informative than individual buys?
- Do positioning extremes predict continuation or reversal?
- Does flow information add value after price momentum is controlled?

## Warning

Never evaluate a delayed disclosure as though it were known on the underlying transaction date.
