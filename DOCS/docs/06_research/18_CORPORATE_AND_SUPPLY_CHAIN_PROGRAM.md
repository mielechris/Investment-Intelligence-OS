# Corporate and Supply Chain Research Program

## Objective

Test how public corporate actions, capex, supplier relationships, contracts, facilities, and supply-chain disruptions affect returns.

## Event Classes

- capex announcement;
- data-center build;
- factory opening;
- government contract;
- supplier agreement;
- customer concentration;
- guidance revision;
- earnings surprise;
- production outage;
- port disruption;
- export restriction.

## Research Questions

- Do supplier stocks react more strongly than customers?
- How long do capex beneficiaries retain excess returns?
- Are publicly announced government relationships already priced?
- How does commodity exposure change margin transmission?
- Which supply-chain relationships are actually material?

## Relationship Integrity

Only evidence-backed relationships may enter the treatment group.

A meeting is not a contract.
