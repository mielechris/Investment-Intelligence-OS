# Weather, Agriculture, Livestock, and Commodity Research Program

## Objective

Test physical-market causal chains from weather, crop conditions, disease, inventories, production, and logistics.

## Domains

- soybeans;
- corn;
- wheat;
- cattle;
- hogs;
- crude oil;
- natural gas;
- copper;
- gold;
- other approved commodities.

## Research Requirements

For agriculture:

- geography;
- crop stage;
- planting/harvest calendar;
- precipitation;
- temperature;
- drought;
- condition;
- yield expectations;
- inventories;
- imports/exports.

For livestock:

- herd size;
- disease;
- feed costs;
- slaughter;
- trade restrictions.

## Research Questions

- Which weather anomalies actually affect yields?
- How much inventory buffers the shock?
- When do futures curves react before spot?
- Which downstream companies gain or lose?
- How quickly does substitution reduce scarcity?

## Failure Control

Weather outside the relevant geography or crop stage should not generate the same treatment label.
