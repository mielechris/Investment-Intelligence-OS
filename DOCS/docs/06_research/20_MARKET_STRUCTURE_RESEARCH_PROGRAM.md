# Market Structure Research Program

## Objective

Measure whether technical and market-structure variables improve timing and risk control.

## Features

- trend;
- breadth;
- realized volatility;
- implied volatility;
- volume;
- liquidity;
- spreads;
- futures term structure;
- options skew;
- open interest;
- gap behavior.

## Research Questions

- Does trend confirmation improve policy-event trades?
- Does high volatility reduce signal quality?
- Does breadth distinguish durable sector moves from isolated names?
- Does options skew contain incremental information?
- When does liquidity make a statistically good signal untradable?

## Baselines

Every complex technical feature must be compared with simple trend and volatility baselines.
