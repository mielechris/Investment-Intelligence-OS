# ADR-008 — Deterministic Risk Veto Authority

**Status:** ACCEPTED  
**Decision owner:** Founder / Architecture  
**Initial decision date:** 2026-08-20  
**Applies to:** V0.1  
**Supersedes:** None  
**Superseded by:** None

---

## Context

Strong narratives and high model confidence must not bypass portfolio constraints.

## Decision Drivers

- correctness;
- auditability;
- risk control;
- implementation speed;
- future institutional readiness;
- reversibility;
- operational simplicity.

## Options Considered

1. Let committee size trades
2. Let AI risk agent approve trades
3. Use deterministic independent Risk Engine with veto

## Decision

Risk Engine has independent deterministic veto authority.

## Rationale

Separates analytical conviction from portfolio suitability and protects capital.

## Positive Consequences

- Clear authority
- Testable invariants
- Safe no-trade path

## Negative Consequences / Trade-Offs

- May reject profitable ideas
- Requires separate risk implementation

## Risks and Controls

| Risk | Control |
|---|---|
| Decision becomes stale | Review trigger and superseding ADR |
| Implementation drifts from decision | Ticket/spec traceability and architecture tests |
| Hidden exception weakens control | Audit exception and explicit approval |
