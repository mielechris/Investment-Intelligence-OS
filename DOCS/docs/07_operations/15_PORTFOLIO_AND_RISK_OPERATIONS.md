# Portfolio and Risk Operations

## Daily Checks

- NAV;
- cash;
- gross exposure;
- net exposure;
- single-position concentration;
- sector concentration;
- causal-cluster concentration;
- volatility;
- liquidity;
- drawdown;
- open invalidations.

## Risk Policy Changes

Require:

- proposed change;
- reason;
- evidence;
- affected strategies;
- scenario test;
- approval;
- new version;
- audit record.

## Veto

Risk veto is final for the current risk decision.

A new risk assessment requires materially changed inputs or an approved policy version.

## Accounting Mismatch

Any unreconciled portfolio mismatch:

- blocks new risk;
- raises critical alert;
- triggers incident review.

## Drawdown States

Each threshold must define:

- warning;
- reduced-risk state;
- stand-down;
- recovery requirements.
