# Post-Close Runbook

## Reconciliation

- compare order intents to paper orders;
- compare orders to fills;
- compare fills to positions;
- compare positions to portfolio snapshot;
- compare cash ledger to account cash.

## Research Capture

Record:

- realized paper outcomes;
- mark-to-market changes;
- catalyst status;
- invalidation status;
- major deviations from expected path.

## Journal

Ensure material:

- decisions;
- no-trades;
- vetoes;
- overrides;
- incidents

are recorded.

## Backup

Verify daily backup or scheduled backup success.

## Daily Summary

Generate:

- portfolio state;
- drawdown;
- exposures;
- open theses;
- closed theses;
- source-health issues;
- model issues;
- incidents;
- lessons.
