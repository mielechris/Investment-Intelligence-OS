# IIOS Living Wall — Museum Master 1.2 artwork statement

IIOS Living Wall is a living architectural portrait of an intelligence factory governed by evidence. Its three-level cutaway keeps all eighteen rooms present at once: Radar, Research, Policy, Macro, External Intelligence, Committee, the Red Room, Risk Inspection, the sealed Paper Execution Bay, Portfolio, Monitoring, Learning, Judgment, Evidence, Thesis, Control, Replay, and Expansion. Each room represents a discipline, a boundary, or a memory—not a promise of activity.

MAX occupies the foreman walkway as the work's dominant witness. MAX is not an autonomous trader. He observes whether evidence has earned movement and whether the house remains inside its authority. When complete governed receipts are absent, the factory does not simulate productivity. It becomes *The House Is Quiet*.

The work consumes the sanitized `living_wall_truth.v1` read model. It presents observation and governed paper-market research only. Telemetry remains read-only; direct ledger, backend-write, trade, and live-execution authority remain false. Financial intelligence is the subject of the artwork, not a grant of financial authority.

Ownership of an edition does not grant trading authority, credentials, private data, source-control access, financial performance guarantees, or live-execution capability. The software makes no claim of guaranteed return, autonomous trading, blockchain title, or NFT ownership.
