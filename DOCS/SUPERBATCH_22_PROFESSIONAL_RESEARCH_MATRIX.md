# Professional Research Source Matrix

| Source family | Initial state | Required gates | Permitted output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Koyfin human cockpit | UNAVAILABLE | License, human capture, point-in-time timestamp, conflict and attribution review | Attributed observation |
| Market Vision | UNVERIFIED | Subscription rights, publication identity, primary corroboration and outcome follow-up | Commodity-method hypothesis |
| Jesse interviews | UNAVAILABLE | Consent, confidentiality exclusion, transcript correction and Jesse approval | Attributed interview claim pending review |
| Public manager commentary | UNAVAILABLE | Official source, rights, disclosure delay, attribution and counterargument | Attributed opinion/hypothesis |
| Public portfolio observations | UNAVAILABLE | Official filing/source, reporting-delay warning and primary verification | Delayed factual observation |
| Public filings/interviews | UNAVAILABLE | Rights, point-in-time availability, evidence location and human approval | Governed claim candidate |

Allowed maturity states are `UNAVAILABLE`, `UNVERIFIED`, `VERIFIED_UNCORROBORATED`, and `INDEPENDENTLY_CORROBORATED`. Professional evidence cannot independently create a candidate, promotion, synthetic sleeve, operational position, recommendation, score, order, broker connection, ledger write, or execution authority.
