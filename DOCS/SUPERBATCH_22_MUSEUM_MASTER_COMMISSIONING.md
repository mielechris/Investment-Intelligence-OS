This batch consolidates the complete read-only Control Room, truthful living-idle Gallery, Tuesday research contracts, 24 independent synthetic comparison sleeves, professional-observation readiness, deterministic rehearsals, and post-close reporting.

This is a source-only, fixture-first paper-research commissioning batch. It does not grant provider, credential, broker, operational paper-order, ledger-write, automatic-promotion, or live-execution authority.

`museum_commissioning.module_registry()` identifies 28 current modules and their latest source, freshness contract, Museum destination, and disposition. Old command shells, polling owners, private-path readers, simulated live activity, and unsafe fallbacks are prohibited rather than restored.
