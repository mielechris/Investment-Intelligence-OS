The Expansion Wing defines ten independently scored, fixture-first research lanes: US equities, equity ETFs, Treasury/rates, bond proxies, commodity proxies, FX proxies, cryptocurrency reference assets, listed options, intraday research, and relative value. Every lane uses the common Opportunity Passport. Timestamps, identifiers, primary-source state, completeness, invalidation, maximum modeled loss, liquidity, volatility, correlation, provenance counts, and governance are validated before projection.

Direct instruments and proxies are never interchangeable. Proxy lanes preserve an explicit proxy basis. Incomplete bond maturity, duration, yield convention, credit quality, callable status, or proxy basis fails closed. Options lacking contract identity, expiration, strike, type, multiplier, bid/ask, implied volatility, Greeks state, assignment risk, or maximum loss are `RESEARCH_ONLY_UNPRICEABLE`. Intraday research has separate session, latency, spread, liquidity, freshness, and market-hours gates.

## Candidate fusion and paper laboratory

Scanner, professional, and historical observations retain separate provenance. Professional opinion alone is insufficient. Fusion reports agreement, contradiction, missing evidence, correlated duplicates, independent-source count, and confidence decomposition. The governed sequence is professional observation → independent evidence → historical testing → candidate → Skeptic → committee → risk → paper test → outcome scoring.

Equal-weight virtual research sleeves compare methods without creating positions. The separate consolidated paper view remains fixed to the existing $10,000 NAV and requires the pre-existing committee/risk gates before any actual paper position. Entries retain timestamp and price provenance, spread, fees, slippage, sizing hypothesis, invalidation, exit rules, holding period, marking, benchmark, excursions, results, and attribution.
