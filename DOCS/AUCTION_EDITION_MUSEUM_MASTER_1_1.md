# IIOS Living Wall — The Auction Edition — Museum Master 1.1

## Artwork and artist/founder statement

IIOS Living Wall is a living architectural portrait of a governed private-intelligence factory. Its rooms make the discipline of evidence visible: research may proceed, dissent may persist, and a quiet house may be the most truthful result. The work was founded and directed as an alternative to the promise-making visual language of financial terminals. It depicts permitted research and governed paper-market activity; it does not promise profit, guaranteed returns, autonomous trading, or live execution.

## Technical and governance description

The medium is a responsive browser artwork driven by the sanitized `living_wall_truth.v1` read model. Exact, complete receipts may illuminate one governed route. Missing lineage, unknown events, stale or conflicting truth, unavailable telemetry, or unsafe authority freeze activity. Ambient light, haze, clocks, gauges, and MAX's quiet observation communicate presence without fabricating work. Ledger, backend-write, trade, and live-execution authority remain false.

## Collector quick start

1. Confirm the display is landscape, 16:9, ventilated, surge protected, and connected to a stable network.
2. Open only the approved immutable or stable Preview URL.
3. Verify the plaque reads `Museum Master 1.1` and Factory Watch shows observation-only authority.
4. Enter browser fullscreen manually and choose Wall Art Mode.
5. Use Pause Scene or the operating-system reduced-motion setting when motion is undesirable.
6. If truth is unavailable, retain the authored fail-closed quiet presentation; do not substitute invented data.

## Exhibition mode and photography

- Conceal controls with Wall Art Mode; browser chrome disappears only after a human confirms fullscreen.
- Keep the complete artwork, edition identity, and safety language within the frame.
- Photograph only the artwork surface. Exclude desktop, browser history, bookmarks, notifications, credentials, operator tools, and unrelated windows.
- Record the deployment URL, commit, viewport, date, display model, crop, and whether motion was paused.
- Never photograph private telemetry, response bodies, headers, bypass values, or configuration screens.

## Conservation and maintenance

Preserve the source commit and provenance manifest, test future browser updates in Preview, inspect quarterly for clipping and burn-in, use scheduled dimming, and retain the fail-closed offline composition. Replace displays with a color-calibrated 16:9 equivalent. Do not silently replace character art, rewrite historical receipts, or enable unsupported authority. Back up certificates separately from the runtime.
