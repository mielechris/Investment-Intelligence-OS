# Investment Intelligence OS — Project Charter

## Mission

Build an AI-powered investment intelligence platform that improves decision quality through evidence, reasoning, historical validation, and disciplined risk management—not hype.

## Vision

Create a personal investment research platform that continuously gathers public information, understands how the global economy is changing, tests investment hypotheses, and helps users make better long-term decisions.

## Version 1 Objectives

- Collect public market and economic data.
- Build a world model of the economy.
- Connect events through cause-and-effect reasoning.
- Generate evidence-based daily investment briefings.
- Simulate trades through paper trading.
- Learn from every decision.

## Core Principles

- Evidence before opinion.
- Every recommendation must be explainable.
- Every thesis must be testable.
- Protect capital before pursuing returns.
- Learn continuously from successes and failures.
- Never trust a single information source.
- Confidence must be earned.

## Major Intelligence Domains

- Markets and economic indicators
- Federal Reserve and monetary policy
- U.S. policy and Congress
- Geopolitics
- Corporates and public investor research
- Commodities, agriculture, weather, and energy
- Supply chains and currencies
- Historical market regimes

## Success Criteria

The platform should enable the user to:

1. Understand what happened today.
2. Explain why it matters.
3. Identify affected industries and companies.
4. Present supporting evidence.
5. Recommend buy, sell, hold, or no action.
6. Explain the rationale and uncertainty behind each recommendation.
7. Record outcomes and learn from them.

## Long-Term Goal

Build an institutional-grade intelligence platform that starts as a personal system, operates with discipline, and develops the capability to produce serious investment insight.
